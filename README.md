## `whoami`

Senior Director of Technology at a healthcare and home care organization in San Francisco. My scope is deliberately wide: **cybersecurity, IT infrastructure, BI/data backend, compliance, and facilities** — the full operational stack of enterprise technology, minus the clean parts.

I care deeply about building systems that move at the pace of innovation *without* outrunning governance and security. In healthcare, that balance isn't optional — it's the job.
